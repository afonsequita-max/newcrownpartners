About NewCrown Partners

NewCrown Partners LLC provides dependable produced water hauling and transportation services throughout Midland, Odessa, and the greater Permian Basin region. Our mission is to support oil and gas operations with reliable logistics solutions that prioritize safety, regulatory compliance, environmental responsibility, and operational excellence.

We believe in building long-term partnerships through professional service, responsive communication, and dependable field support tailored to the evolving needs of energy operators and service companies.
